a make.fun entry by [xxstealz](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36).

## The line (95 characters)

> My laptop caught a virus from my smart fridge, and now we're both quarantined. I can't come in.

The absurd chain is the joke: the laptop caught a virus from the smart
fridge, so both of them are quarantined — half believable as a sick-day
text, and the twist lands in the last sentence.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` —
exactly one line, 95 characters, no trailing newline — and validates the
1–200 character gate before writing.
